Reliable quantities come first.

The calculation uses the saved recipe and chosen additions. The initial chicken example uses 400 g per portion, or 800 g for two. Review the quantities shown in the current recipe.

Each saved order keeps its recipe quantities. Editing a recipe affects new orders, and leaves earlier orders consistent.

  1. Use measured quantities and consistent units.
  2. Allow for preparation yield in the recipe.
  3. Record waste and compare physical stock regularly.
